I used to take <em>a lot of photos</em>, then I stopped. This is an attempt to get back into taking photos.

In an attempt to improve my photography, I've started carrying a camera around. The act of carrying a camera makes your actively aware of opportunities for photos. This is fundamentally differnt from just having your phone camera on you at all times. I'm carrying a Fujifilm XQ1, which is pretty old as far as digital cameras go, but it has some great features, and it's about as small as it gets.

I'm trying to post shots each month. I'm hoping this will encourage me to take more photos.
